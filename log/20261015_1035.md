## Skill improvements found by the pilot (already folded in)

- Rubric:
  - no A–D cycling;
  - vary the number of wrong options in multis;
  - numeric distractors trace to a named misconception;
  - no complement or twin of the key;
  - "Suppose" for hypotheticals;
  - stems can leak across items;
  - check dataset coverage;
  - chapter prose can be conceptually wrong.
- Validator: the key-sequence and wrong-option-count checks.
- `test_quiz.py`: the math check looks only at the visible stem and options; `fast_forward` takes milliseconds.
- Fact-check template: Python `round()` rounds halves to even; timestamp `month` columns.
