# Centered, symmetric three-badge illustration

User requested a redesign because unequal connector lengths and badge placement made the artwork feel off-center and asymmetric.

## Final design

- Mirrored AI (left) and Cloud Computing (right) badges have matching card dimensions, alignment, spacing and connector treatment.
- The laptop and Python badge share the vertical center line. Python is centered below the laptop to remove the extra right-side weight.
- All three callouts follow one short straight connector system, based on 40-pixel geometry. AI reaches the neural brain; Cloud Computing reaches the smooth cloud backdrop; Python reaches the centered laptop front edge.
- The cover-inspired neural brain remains the dominant foreground subject, with balanced cloud lobes behind it and the navy/cyan/violet/pink palette.
