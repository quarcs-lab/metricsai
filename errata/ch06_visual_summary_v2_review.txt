Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-06-01: Homoskedasticity is illustrated by parallel bands around the
  conditional mean with equal vertical spread at three x values. The bands are
  labeled as the mean plus/minus the error standard deviation, not a confidence
  interval or a boundary containing all observations.
- VS-06-02: Matched side-by-side plots use the same observation and x position.
  The error u_i ends at the solid population line; the residual e_i ends at the
  dashed fitted line. Correct formulas and color-matched legends identify both.
- VS-06-03: Slope variance is conditional: Var(b2 | X) = sigma_u^2 / Sxx.
  X is defined as all observed regressors, and Sxx is defined explicitly.
- VS-06-04: Assumptions now include positive regressor variation, conditional
  zero-mean errors, positive constant conditional variance, and conditionally
  uncorrelated errors. Sufficient additional asymptotic conditions are i.i.d.
  observations, finite fourth moments, and positive finite regressor variance.

Additional mathematical and visual checks:
- Population model, conditional mean, fitted line and least-squares criterion
  have correct subscripts and distinguish unknown beta from estimated b.
- Unbiased, Efficient (BLUE), and Consistent are all explicitly labeled.
  Efficiency is restricted to LINEAR unbiased estimators. Consistency is
  qualified by additional asymptotic conditions and uses probability convergence.
- Repeated-sample illustrations show differing estimated slopes.
- Estimated slope SE is s_e/sqrt(Sxx), with residual variance estimator
  s_e^2 = sum(e_i^2)/(n-2), n>2.
- CLT uses sqrt(n)(b2-beta2) converging in distribution to a centered normal
  law with variance sigma_u^2/Var(x), under the stated conditions. It does not
  claim an exact finite-sample normal law for arbitrary error distributions.
