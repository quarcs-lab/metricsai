Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-08-01: Beta measures signed market exposure, not total return volatility.
  The covariance/market-variance formula and total-variance decomposition are
  displayed, including zero covariance between the residual and market return.
  Positive, subunit positive, and negative exposures are distinguished.
- VS-08-02: CAPM regression explicitly uses stock and market excess returns
  over the risk-free return. Zero alpha is a CAPM prediction and testable null.
- VS-08-03: Percentage points replace relative percentage-change language.
  Further source inspection found the old summary reverses the regression used
  in the chapter: the chapter predicts GDP growth from unemployment-rate change.
  Therefore, instead of retaining the audit's illustrative reverse-direction
  slope of -0.5, this candidate uses the chapter's actual fitted equation:
  predicted growth = 3.01 - 1.59 * unemployment-rate change.
  Interpretation compares two predictions and does not assert causation.
- VS-08-04: Causal claims require credible identification and assumptions;
  adding regressors is neither necessary nor sufficient. Random assignment
  illustrates the importance of design.
