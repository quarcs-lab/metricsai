Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-10-01: Removed the false decorative equation yhat=e^2. The fitted-value
  formula is yhat=Xb, accompanied by the valid least-squares objective.
- VS-10-02: Adjusted R-squared is 1 - [RSS/(n-k)]/[TSS/(n-1)], with balanced
  delimiters. k is explicitly the number of coefficients including the intercept.
- VS-10-03: Near collinearity means a regressor is nearly a linear combination
  of the others. Pairwise correlations can miss it; auxiliary R_j^2 near 1 is
  identified as a warning.
- VS-10-04: On the same outcome and sample, adding regressors gives
  R-squared_full >= R-squared_restricted. It may stay unchanged; the graph's
  ordinary R-squared curve never decreases.

Additional mathematical and visual checks:
- The additive fitted model, squared-residual objective, e_i=y_i-yhat_i,
  normal equations X'e=0, and zero residual sum with intercept are correct.
- Full column rank and n>k are stated for the displayed estimation and fit
  formulas. Perfect collinearity is rank(X)<k, preventing unique estimation of
  all coefficients. Fahrenheit/Celsius with an intercept is a correct example.
- Partial coefficient interpretation holds other included regressors fixed
  and is explicitly for the additive linear model. Pairwise association is
  distinguished from partial association and from causation.
- FWL removes the same other regressors and intercept from both x_j and y.
  Its residual regression has slope sum(r_xi*r_yi)/sum(r_xi^2), equal to the
  corresponding full-model coefficient. The illustration was refined to show
  residuals on both sides of zero and a fitted line through the origin.
- RSS and centered TSS are correctly defined. R-squared uses an intercept
  and positive TSS. Adjusted R-squared may fall or be negative.
- Regression standard error is sqrt(RSS/(n-k)), labeled residual spread in
  y units rather than a universal prediction-error measure.
- Information criteria are described as fit/complexity tradeoffs, with lower
  values preferred only in comparable analyses. Outcome, sample and compatible
  likelihood qualifications are explicit, as is predictive evaluation.
- Reporting includes coefficients, SEs, test statistics, p-values, CIs, sample
  size, fit measures and the overall F-test of joint slope significance.
- Approved mind-map style, rectangular borders and explanatory diagrams retained.
