- VS-07-01: Corrected t = (b2 - beta_2,0)/SE(b2), dividing by SE only once.
- VS-07-02: Exact t_(n-2) inference under H0 explicitly requires normal,
  homoskedastic, independent errors conditional on X and classical SEs.
  Robust inference instead uses an asymptotic or appropriately adjusted
  reference distribution.
- VS-07-03: Conditional mean-zero E[u | X] = 0 is explicit. The image distinguishes
  unchanged numerical coefficients when switching only the SE estimator from
  changes in OLS sampling variance and efficiency under heteroskedasticity or
  dependence. Robust SEs are not presented as a remedy for a biased model.
- VS-07-04: Statistical significance is evidence against H0 under the test
  assumptions, not proof of an effect or causation.
