Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-09-01: Semi-elasticity is the derivative of log y with respect to x, b2.
  Percentage interpretation includes the factor 100: exact fitted-curve change
  100(exp(b2*Delta x)-1), approximately 100*b2*Delta x for small absolute changes.
- VS-09-02: A 1% increase in x in the linear-log curve changes y by
  b2*ln(1.01), approximately b2/100. Equality and approximation are distinguished.
- VS-09-03: Expansions use small absolute h, with |h| much less than 1 and the
  real-log domain h>-1. Growth approximation similarly uses small absolute r.
- VS-09-04: Removed the cross-outcome R-squared ranking. Direct R-squared
  comparisons require the same dependent variable and sample; comparisons of
  level and log-outcome models should evaluate predictions on a common scale.

Additional mathematical and visual checks:
- Natural log base, positive domain, fractional-change definition, exact log
  ratio, and factor 100 for percentage change are correct.
- Natural-log graph is a qualitative increasing concave curve for positive x,
  anchored at (1,0). Misleading numerical tick spacings were removed.
- Log-log elasticity derivative and exact finite 1% change formula are shown;
  the familiar b2-percent interpretation is marked approximate.
- Coefficient interpretations explicitly refer to fitted curves, avoiding an
  automatic conditional arithmetic-mean interpretation for log-outcome models.
- Compound growth's exact log transform uses t*ln(1+r), with decimal rate r.
  The approximation uses r*t. Exponential and straight-line diagrams have
  correctly labeled level and log axes.
- Doubling time is ln(2)/ln(1+r), r>0. The Rule of 72 is explicitly approximate,
  with its percentage-valued denominator defined as 100*r.
- Earnings slope 0.131 is an illustrative value from the chapter, not a new
  empirical estimate. Exact fitted-curve transformation is distinguished from
  the percentage approximation and from a causal claim.
- Back-transformation warning distinguishes exponentiated log predictions from
  predictions of arithmetic mean y, which generally need an adjustment.
- Rectangular borders, branching layout and approved palette retained.
