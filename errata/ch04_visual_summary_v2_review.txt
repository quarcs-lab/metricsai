Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-04-01: Two-sided graph has separate negative and positive cutoffs (-c, c),
  not plus/minus at both points. c is explicitly the 1-alpha/2 t quantile.
  Each rejection tail has mass alpha/2. One-sided graphs shade the correct
  single tail with mass alpha and use d = t_(nu,1-alpha).
- VS-04-02: Exact t distribution is conditional on i.i.d. normal sampling with
  nu = n-1. Nonnormal inference is identified as a large-sample approximation,
  requiring i.i.d. draws and finite positive variance.
- VS-04-03: Removed the universal t-interval claim. Proportions use a normal
  critical value in an explicitly large-sample Wald interval. Independent
  observations and adequate counts are stated; small counts need another method.

Additional content and visual checks:
- SE = s/sqrt(n); random t pivot uses S and Xbar, while observed test statistic
  uses xbar, the null mean mu_0, and the estimated SE.
- Confidence interval uses xbar +/- t_(nu,1-alpha/2) SE with quantile convention
  defined. Its illustration correctly marks the margin from center to endpoint.
- 95% confidence is explained through coverage over repeated samples, with
  exact versus approximate qualifications.
- p-value definition refers to a test statistic at least as extreme under H0.
  Reject/fail-to-reject rules are correct, with the warning that failing to
  reject does not establish H0. Alpha and direction are selected in advance.
- The proportion CI uses estimated p in its SE. The null z test instead uses
  p_0 in its SE. Both formulas are visually correct.
- The t5 versus standard-normal illustration has been refined to show similar
  central peaks and heavier t tails; it is a hand-drawn schematic, not a
  numerical plotting artifact.
- Reporting includes statistical versus practical importance, sample size,
  estimates, dispersion, SE, CI, hypotheses, test statistic, and p-value.
- Central mind-map structure, rectangular borders and approved colors retained.
