Audit corrections implemented:
- VS-11-01: Full column rank, n>k and the intercept-inclusive k convention are
  explicit. Exact classical t/F laws additionally require conditional normal
  errors with covariance sigma^2 I. Robust inference has an appropriate
  asymptotic or adjusted reference law. The two-sided 5% cutoff is the 97.5th
  percentile of t_(n-k); about 2 is described only as a rough large-sample cutoff.
- VS-11-02: The slope SE formula is labeled classical homoskedastic. The
  residualized x_j is defined by regressing x_j on all other columns of X,
  including the intercept. It is explicitly not merely mean-centered x_j.
  Residual variance s_e^2 = sum(e_i^2)/(n-k) is defined.
- VS-11-03: Adjusted R-squared increases iff the classical partial F statistic
  for the added regressors exceeds 1, on the same outcome and sample with
  positive TSS. For one added regressor, F_added=t_classical^2. These identities
  are explicitly distinguished from robust t/F statistics and the overall test.

Additional mathematical and visual checks:
- Conditional mean-zero and covariance assumptions distinguish unbiasedness
  and BLUE from exact distributional inference. Consistency additionally
  requires suitable sampling, moment and design conditions.
- Unbiased, Efficient (BLUE), and Consistent are explicitly labeled; efficiency
  is restricted to LINEAR unbiased estimators.
- Residualized-regressor illustrations show little versus more variation around
  zero. The precision discussion qualifies larger SE as holding other factors fixed.
- Individual t statistic subtracts the hypothesized coefficient once and divides
  once by its SE. Confidence interval and two-sided critical regions match.
  Each t tail is labeled 0.025, with separate negative and positive cutoffs.
- Partial F formula uses ((RSS_R-RSS_U)/q)/(RSS_U/(n-k_U)); restricted and
  unrestricted models, q, RSS, and the full-model coefficient count are defined.
- Overall null sets all non-intercept coefficients to zero, giving q=k-1.
- F diagram has nonnegative support and a single right-tail rejection region.
- Fit improvement is distinguished from 5% statistical significance.
- Reporting includes estimates, SE method, confidence intervals and p-values.
